Company Details

Vylex (Pty) Ltd is a South African technology group structured to deliver multi-disciplinary digital solutions. In accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), we are committed to protecting your privacy.

  • Usage: Data is used solely for communication and service delivery. We do not sell data to third parties.

In accordance with the Promotion of Access to Information Act, Vylex maintains a PAIA manual detailing records held by the company.

Requests for access to records should be directed to our Information Officer at legal@vylex.co.za using Form C as prescribed by the Act.
